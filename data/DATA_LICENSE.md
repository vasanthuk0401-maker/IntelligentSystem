# Data attribution

`jobs_linkedin_sample.jsonl` is a random 4,000-posting subset of
**LinkedIn Job Postings (2023 - 2024)** by Arsh Koneru and collaborators
(https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arshkon/linkedin-job-postings), licensed under
**CC BY-SA 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes: rows sampled, HTML stripped, descriptions truncated to 5,000 characters,
fields renamed and salary/remote/experience fields reformatted by `import_jobs.py`.
This derived subset is shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

`jobs.json` (24 jobs) and `sample_cv.txt` are fictional examples written for this project.
